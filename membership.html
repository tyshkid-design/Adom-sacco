Join ADOM SACCO

Become part of a faith-driven financial community. Affordable, transparent, and built on Christian values. Your membership journey starts here.

Eligibility

Who Can Join ADOM SACCO?

Membership is open to all professing Christians and institutions aligned with our faith-based values. We welcome individuals, families, groups, and organizations.

👤

Individual Members

Any Kenyan Christian aged 18 and above with a valid National ID or Passport. Employed, self-employed, or business owners welcome.

⛪

Churches & Ministries

Registered churches, ministries, and Christian organizations. Open a group account to manage tithes, offerings, and ministry funds.

👥

Groups & Chamas

Welfare groups, merry-go-rounds, investment clubs, and faith-based chamas with a minimum of 5 members can register collectively.

Documentation Required

What You Need to Bring

🪪
National ID / Passport

A clear copy of your current Kenyan National ID card or Passport. Must be valid and legible.

📋
KRA PIN Certificate

Your Kenya Revenue Authority PIN certificate. You can download it from the KRA iTax portal for free.

📸
Passport Photograph

One recent passport-size colour photograph with a white background. Digital copies accepted.

💰
Initial Payment

KSh 3,500 total: Registration (KSh 500) + Min. Shares (KSh 2,000) + First Contribution (KSh 1,000).

📱
Active Mobile Number

A valid Safaricom / Airtel / Telkom number registered in your name for M-Pesa transactions and SMS alerts.

✉️
Email Address

A working email address for account statements, communications, and your online member portal access.
