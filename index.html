Who We Are

A SACCO Built on Faith & Integrity

ADOM SACCO empowers Christian communities through smart savings and credit solutions grounded in integrity and compassion. We believe financial empowerment and faith can walk hand in hand.

Our Vision

To economically empower members through convenient access to affordable finance within a Christ-centered community.

Our Mission

To strategically build a strong SACCO responding to the needs of members confessing the Christian faith.

Our Core Values

People First

We value and empower our members and staff, fostering a caring community of shared purpose.

⚖️
Integrity & Transparency

Honesty and accountability in every action, every relationship, every shilling.

🌱
Innovation

Embracing creativity and modern solutions to meet the evolving needs of our members.

Community & Teamwork

Collaborating with unity and mutual respect to achieve our shared vision and goals.

How to Join

Become a Member in 3 Simple Steps

Join a supportive, faith-led community and enjoy fair, affordable financial services. The process is simple, transparent, and fast.

1

Complete the Membership Form

Fill in our online membership application form with your personal details. Takes less than 10 minutes. You can also visit our office at Highview Plaza, Kiambu Road.

2

Submit KYC Documents

Provide a copy of your National ID or Passport, KRA PIN certificate, and one passport-size photograph. Group/church accounts require additional documentation.

3

Pay Initial Fees & Start Saving

Pay the registration fee and minimum share capital via M-Pesa or bank transfer. Set up your standing order for monthly contributions and start your journey!
